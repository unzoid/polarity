Swift packages in Polarity for Apple TV

The app is built with supabase-swift (signing in and reading the account's settings) and the
packages it builds in. Each one's licence text, and the NOTICE file of each Apache-2.0 package,
is in this folder, taken from the version named here:

  supabase-swift 2.55.6 (MIT), github.com/supabase/supabase-swift
  swift-crypto 4.5.1 (Apache-2.0), github.com/apple/swift-crypto
  swift-asn1 1.3.1 (Apache-2.0), github.com/apple/swift-asn1
  swift-http-types 1.3.1 (Apache-2.0), github.com/apple/swift-http-types
  swift-concurrency-extras 1.4.1 (MIT), github.com/pointfreeco/swift-concurrency-extras
  swift-clocks 1.1.0 (MIT), github.com/pointfreeco/swift-clocks
  xctest-dynamic-overlay 1.11.0 (MIT), github.com/pointfreeco/xctest-dynamic-overlay

When apple-tv/project.yml moves supabase-swift to another version, check the packages it builds
in (its Package.swift) and update these files.

FONTLOG for Polarity Sans

Basic font information
----------------------
Polarity Sans is a modified version of TikTok Sans, released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Its proportions, widths and spacing were fitted to measurements of typefaces the requester chose. Every outline comes from the open base. No outline, point or kerning pair was taken from any other font.

Use: ui. Scripts: latin.

Files: ttf/PolaritySans-Regular.ttf, webfonts/PolaritySans-Regular.woff2, ttf/PolaritySans-Medium.ttf, webfonts/PolaritySans-Medium.woff2, ttf/PolaritySans-SemiBold.ttf, webfonts/PolaritySans-SemiBold.woff2, ttf/PolaritySans-Bold.ttf, webfonts/PolaritySans-Bold.woff2, ttf/PolaritySans-ExtraBold.ttf, webfonts/PolaritySans-ExtraBold.woff2, OFL.txt, FONTLOG.txt, fonts.css, README.md
